Magical AI Through Entropy

There were some great articles recently about DeepMind and the brilliance of Move 37. When you dig into the math of it, it's fascinating. There were many "logical" and at the time "better" moves, but when the model starts to take the path less traveled and "explore" other options through weights in its reasoning, it can occasionally come up with something novel precisely because it was less likely. Interestingly, Go players had gotten stuck in their ways and all started to play like each other, following known conventions, and Move 37 woke them up and their move patterns started to change with new techniques.

Also let's not kid ourselves that cold outreach is a human mastered skill. There are plenty of cold outreaches that aren't AI and still infuriate the recipient. The human ones that work could be the needle in the haystack, but they could also have a whole host of circumstances outside of the email copy itself that happened to make it effective in that instance or moment.
