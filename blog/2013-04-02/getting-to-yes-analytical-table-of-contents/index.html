I took two negotiation classes in college, and they were both my favorite of the dozens that I took while at Stanford. While two classes do not make me an expert in negotiation, I have used the lessons I learned in them, as well as from my short life, many times. I've even helped friends get through tough talks by passing on my collective knowledge. One of the best tools, my favorite book from ES250: Negotiation was Getting to Yes.

I read every word and scrupiously took notes in the margins when I needed them the most. It's a quick read. I'm a slow reader and I finished it in a couple of days. If you don't have time to read it, I HIGHLY suggest looking over the Analytical Table of Contents, reproduced below.

Adapted from: Getting to Yes: Negotiating Agreement Without Giving In – Roger Fisher & William Ury
