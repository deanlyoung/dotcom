> I think of sensory data systems having three stages. Gather, understand, and action. What I love about the LVT (LiveView Technologies) guardgate is you get all three stages in one solution. A camera on it's own, an intercom on it's own, and a barrier on it's own are only pieces of a puzzle. Being able to deploy anywhere with no construction or high install costs is what makes this a game changer in delivering Intelligent Site Management.

<https://lnkd.in/p/gaQCDRKg>

The one qualm I have with "understand" is that it implies adding more work. I must analyze and derive my own insights. Therefore, I prefer "insights" because the product should do the heavy lifting and not make more work for me.

This might sound a lot like outsourcing our judgment and thinking to AI (which I am not an advocate of), but it is different because we are talking about buying a product or service for a specific project or task. People don't buy things to make more work for themselves (i.e. create a new job to be done). They want to save time in a particular area (maybe repetitive, mundane, or not their core focus).
